My three guiding principles 

Faith in Action: Spiritual development relies on active engagement rather than passive observation. Belief becomes a living force when paired with intentional daily choices, consistent habits, and practical application, this principle serves as a foundational cornerstone because true conviction demands tangible expression through daily duties, personal accountability and ongoing spiritual disciplines
# we start by defining what is faith, Hebrews 11:1 gives us a clear definition of what is fail
#  3 Nephi 13:19–22 we have heard were Jesus is telling his disciples about faith

Integrity and Moral Courage: This principle involves committing to a strict alignment between inward convictions and outward actions, especially in quiet, unobserved moments or when facing external pressure. It acts as a personal pillar, reminding me that enduring peace and spiritual grounding stem from honest, transparent living and uncompromised personal accountability 
# In regards to the principle of integrity, i shall follow the right procedure of doing my work, staying honest and i shall keep my word when no one is watching 
# Proverbs 28:6 also guides about moral uprightness 

Compassionate Service: Cultivating a genuine love for others by actively seeking ways to uplift, support, and serve without expectation of reward or recognition, this principle forms a core cornerstone of my spiritual work, anchoring my daily interaction in empathy and reinforcing the truth that small, consistent acts of kindness drive true spiritual maturity 
# Colossians 3:12, this scripture talks about compassion, humility, gentleness and patience that am expected to display in my compassionate service principle 
# Reflection
Reflecting on my daily journey, my spiritual development relies on active engagement rather than passive observation, finding true conviction through practical application and personal accountability in both my personal and working life. As I navigate my duties, I am continually guided by the principle of integrity and moral courage, striving to maintain a strict alignment between my inward convictions and outward actions even in quiet, unobserved moments. In a professional context, this translates to following the right procedures, maintaining complete honesty, and keeping my word when no one is watching. Grounded by these standards and inspired by the principles of moral uprightness true peace and grounding stem from transparent living and uncompromised personal accountability
